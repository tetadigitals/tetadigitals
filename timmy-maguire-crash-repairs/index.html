Helpful to have to hand

Before you call.

None of this is required to get started, but it makes the first call quicker.

  • Your insurer's name and your policy number
  • A claim reference, if you already have one
  • A few clear photos of the damage
  • Your car's make, model and registration
  • When you can drop the car in
Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes. Insurance work is handled. Call 091 755 998 with your claim details to get started.

Can I pay privately instead?

Yes. Not every job goes through insurance. Ask Timmy for a price.

Will I have a car while mine is in?

A courtesy car has been available. Ask when you book whether one is free for your dates.

Which insurers do you work with?

Ask Timmy directly when you call. He can tell you how it works with your insurer.
