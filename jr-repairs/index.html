FAQ

Questions, answered.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Hours can change on bank holidays, so call ahead.

Are you an MOT test centre?

Yes. JR Repairs qualified as an MOT station in March 2018 and is listed for Class 4 MOT tests, which covers cars and light vans.

Do you work on vans?

Yes. Customers bring both cars and vans for MOTs and repairs.

Can you help if I break down on holiday?

Call 01540 662988. Visitors have been helped at short notice, even on busy days, though it depends on the workload.

Do you fit tyres?

Yes, tyre fitting is one of the services listed with the Scottish Motor Trade Association. Call with your tyre size.
