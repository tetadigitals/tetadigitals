Our story · Born and bred in Allendale

From an old van to The Shed.

Jill's Catering is run by Jill Wilkinson, who grew up in Allendale and has been feeding the valleys for well over a decade.

  1. c.2009

    The van

    Jill starts selling her home-made food from an old van her dad refurbished, cooking everything in her own kitchen.

  2. Growing

    A purpose-built kitchen

    As the business grows, Jill has a purpose-built kitchen built next to her house in Catton.

  3. The Shed

    A tea room is born

    The kitchen becomes a small tea room, known to those who work there as "The Shed" because of its unusual shape.

  4. 2019

    Ten years

    Jill's Catering celebrates its tenth anniversary.

  5. Today

    Tea room, catering and deliveries

    A weekend tea room, outside catering for local occasions, and frozen ready meals delivered around the area during the week.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is the tea room open?

Friday 10am to 4pm, Saturday 10am to 4pm and Sunday 11am to 4pm. Closed Monday to Thursday.

Do you cater for funerals?

Yes. Jill provides outside catering for funerals, as well as local celebrations and parties across the Allen Valleys.

Do you deliver meals?

Yes, home-made frozen ready meals are delivered to people in the local area during the week. Call 07957 571885 to check current delivery areas and days.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. There's outdoor seating as well as all-weather seating, all with countryside views.

How do I book catering?

Tell Jill the date, numbers and occasion by phone, email or Facebook. She'll suggest a spread, and you confirm by phone or email.
