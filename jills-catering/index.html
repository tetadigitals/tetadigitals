FAQ

Questions, answered.

When is the tea room open?

Friday 10am to 4pm, Saturday 10am to 4pm and Sunday 11am to 4pm. Closed Monday to Thursday.

Do you cater for funerals?

Yes. Jill provides outside catering for funerals, as well as local celebrations and parties across the Allen Valleys.

Do you deliver meals?

Yes, home-made frozen ready meals are delivered to people in the local area during the week. Call 07957 571885 to check current delivery areas and days.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. There's outdoor seating as well as all-weather seating, with countryside views.

How do I book catering?

Tell Jill the date, numbers and occasion by phone, email or Facebook. She'll suggest a spread, and you confirm by phone or email.
