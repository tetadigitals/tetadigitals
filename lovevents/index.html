Welcome to LovEvents

A celebration should feel like yours, down to the last linen.

LovEvents is the banquet hall SeBrina and Chris Love built after years of decorating other people's rented halls. Today their own venue on Broadway Street in North Anderson holds four rooms, a catering kitchen and the decor collection SeBrina has gathered over a career in furniture and interior design.

That means one team listens to your vision, sets the room, serves the meal and makes sure the evening feels the way you pictured it. No juggling vendors, no guessing whether the tablecloths will match the flowers.

On-site catering

The meal is where a party slows down and becomes a memory.

At LovEvents the catering happens right here, in the same building as your celebration. There is no outside caterer arriving late, no hand-off between vendors, and no wondering whether the food will match the feel of the room. The people who set your tables are the same people who serve them.

About LovEvents

A family business, built on Love.

Married 31 years and partners in business, SeBrina and Chris Love bring a lifetime of design and hospitality to every event.

The Love story

From furniture showrooms to banquet halls.

For over 15 years, SeBrina and Chris Love worked in the furniture and interior decor industry, owning several furniture stores along the way. They learned how a room makes people feel, and how the right piece in the right place can change everything.

SeBrina carried that eye into events. She began meeting clients to understand how they envisioned their celebration, then decorated their rental halls with decor she had inventoried and rented herself. Word spread, one beautiful room at a time.

Then the couple relocated from Indianapolis to Anderson and opened LovEvents Hall with four rooms of their own. Today their staff can run up to four events at once, and they still offer linen and decor rentals for events at their venue or anywhere needed.
