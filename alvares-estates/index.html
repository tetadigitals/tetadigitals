Renters' Rights Act 2026

What changes for landlords on 1 May 2026.

The biggest shift in renting for a generation, in plain English.

  • Rolling periodic tenanciesFrom 1 May 2026, fixed terms give way to rolling tenancies.
  • Rent rises once a yearIncreases only by Section 13 notice, once a year.
  • No more Section 21No-fault evictions end; possession follows set grounds.
  • One month in advance, at mostRent in advance is capped at one month.

We'll guide you through every change.

Tenant FAQs

Questions renters ask first.

Can I view outside 9–5?

Yes — we arrange evening and weekend viewings, popular with healthcare and shift workers.

How quickly do you deal with repairs?

Fast and personally. Report a repair through the client portal or just call us.

How much deposit will I pay?

A holding deposit of one week's rent, then a tenancy deposit of five weeks' rent (six weeks' if annual rent is £50,000 or more), protected once you move in.

What changed on 1 May 2026?

Rolling periodic tenancies, once-a-year rent reviews by Section 13 notice, and no more than one month's rent in advance.
