FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Brae Coachworks provide a free estimate for repairs to your vehicle.

Can I get a quote from photos?

Photos help, but the team always prefer to see the car in person, because there can be hidden damage.

Do I pay anything up front?

If the repair needs new parts, a deposit is taken before they are ordered.

How soon can you fit me in?

The workshop is often booked weeks ahead. Call 01383 625400 or check Facebook for the next available date.

When can I bring the car for an estimate?

Monday to Thursday, 8am to 4pm, and Friday mornings. Call ahead to check, especially on Fridays and around holidays.
