Signs you need one

Your car will usually tell you.

Pulling

The vehicle drifts left or right on a straight, level road.

Off-center wheel

The steering wheel sits crooked when you drive straight.

Uneven wear

Tires wear faster on the inside or outside edge.

After a hit

A pothole, curb or new tires and suspension parts are good reasons to check.
