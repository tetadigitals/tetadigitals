[ 05 ] WHILE YOU WAIT

What to do while you wait.

General safety advice. If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911 first.

[ 01 ]

Get as far off the road as you can

Pull onto the shoulder or a lot if the car still rolls.

[ 02 ]

Hazards on

At night, keep interior or parking lights on too so you can be seen.

[ 03 ]

Stay clear of traffic

Exit on the side away from the road. If it is safer, wait off the roadway, away from the car.

[ 04 ]

Know where you are

Note the highway, the nearest mile marker, exit or crossroad, and your direction.

[ 05 ]

Keep your phone charged

Save battery for the call back and keep the line free.
