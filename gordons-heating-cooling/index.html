/ 03 Warning signs

Signs your heat needs attention.

General signs worth a call. If anything smells of gas or a CO alarm sounds, leave the building first.

Uneven rooms

Some rooms stay cold while others overheat.

New noises

Banging, squealing or rattling at startup or while running.

Short cycling

The system turns on and off far more often than it used to.

Rising bills

Energy costs climb without a change in how you use the heat.

Odd smells or soot

Burning smells that linger, or soot near the equipment.

Yellow burner flame

On gas equipment, a flame that is not a steady blue is worth checking.

/ 02 Why carbon monoxide matters

You cannot see it or smell it.

/ INVISIBLE

Odorless and colorless

Carbon monoxide gives no warning you can see or smell, which is why screening and alarms matter.

/ ALARMS

Install CO alarms

Put carbon monoxide alarms in the home, especially near sleeping areas, and test them regularly.

/ EMERGENCY

Alarm sounds and people feel ill?

Get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911. Do not go back in until it is declared safe.

/ 03 Filter tips

The cheapest maintenance there is.

Check monthly

Look at the filter every month during heavy heating and cooling use.

Replace when dirty

Follow the filter maker's guidance; pets, dust and construction shorten its life.

Right size, right way

Match the size printed on the old filter and point the airflow arrow toward the equipment.

/ 04 Ductwork

The part of the system you never see.

Ducts carry every bit of heated and cooled air through the house. Ductwork service is part of the full service work Gordon's does.

Signs ducts need a look

  • Rooms that never reach the set temperature
  • Weak airflow at some vents
  • Dust that returns quickly
  • Whistling or rattling in the ducts
