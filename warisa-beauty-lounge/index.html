The edit · No. 54

A calm little treatment room on Talbot Street, dedicated to one thing: making you look and feel good.

Warisa's Beauty Lounge sits within Scruples Hair Design on the principal Talbot Street, and caters to all of your beauty needs, including lashes, nails, waxing and facials.

Whether you are after dramatic eyelash extensions, a long-lasting and immaculate Shellac manicure or a flawless look for something special, the aim is the same: bring out your beauty and your happiness.

It is close to the IFSC, a short walk from O'Connell Street and right next to Connolly station. Ideal for a quick wax on your lunch break, a bit of glam after work, or a weekend appointment while you shop in town.

FAQ

Good to know.

Where exactly are you?

Inside Scruples Hair Design at 54 Talbot Street, Dublin 1, D01 W6E5. It is right next to Connolly station and a short walk from O'Connell Street.

How do I book?

Book online through Treatwell at any time, or call or text 085 854 5729. You can also message on Facebook or Instagram.

When are you open?

Tuesday and Wednesday 10:00 to 17:30, Thursday 12:00 to 19:00, Friday 10:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 16:00. Closed Sunday and Monday.

How long do lash extensions last?

The full set is listed as lasting around four weeks and natural lashes around two weeks. Refills are from €43 at three weeks.

Do you wax men?

Yes, men's treatments are listed from €15. Intimate waxing is for women only.
