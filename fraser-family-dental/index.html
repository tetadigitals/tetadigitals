    About the clinic

    Modern dentistry, personal care.

    At Fraser Family Dental Clinic, located in Vancouver BC, our goal is to provide the best dental care to our patients and within our community. Our office combines the latest dental technology with our highly qualified staff to create a personalized treatment plan for each of our patients. We strive to take the time to get to know our patients and their dental goals so that we may strategize on the best treatment options available.

    “From the moment our patients walk into our office we strive to provide professional and personalized care.”
    KVDentist & owner

    Dr. Kashyap Vora, BDS, FDS RCS (Eng)

    Dr. Kashyap Vora trained in hospital oral surgery in the UK and at Toronto's Hospital for Sick Children, and holds a Fellowship in Dental Surgery from the Royal College of Surgeons of England.     Questions

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Do you bill insurance directly?

    Yes, most major plans. We also accept the CDCP.

    How soon can a new patient be seen?

    We aim for within 48 hours. Dental pain is seen promptly.

    Can I be sedated for a cleaning?

    Yes. IV sedation is available for most procedures, including cleanings.

    Do you see children?

    Yes, adults and children.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Call ahead if you can; we do our best to see walk-in patients.

    What languages do you speak?

    English, Punjabi and Hindi.

    Do you need a referral for implants or wisdom teeth?

    Usually not. Dr. Vora's surgical training means most procedures are done in-house.

    Is whitening safe?

    Yes. It uses a safe ingredient recognized by the dental community.

    How much whiter will my teeth get?

    Up to 8 shades lighter. Results vary from person to person.

    Will whitening work on caps, veneers or bonding?

    Generally not. Whitening works on natural teeth.

    Who shouldn't whiten?

    Anyone allergic to the whitening gel. Please tell us if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

    How do I keep whitening results?

    Limit tea, coffee and red wine.
