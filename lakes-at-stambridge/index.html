FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need to book?

Please call to book to avoid disappointment, especially at weekends. Bookings are by phone on 01702 258274.

When is last food order?

Last food orders are at 3pm. We close at 4pm.

Are you open Monday?

No, we're closed on Monday and Tuesday. We're open Wednesday to Sunday, 9am to 4pm.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's plenty of parking on site at Stambridge Fisheries.

Is the deck heated?

Yes. The large decking area over the lake is covered, with heaters for the winter.
