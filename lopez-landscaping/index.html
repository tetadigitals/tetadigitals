( 04 ) Sod care

Help new sod take root.

General tips. Ask Luis what fits your yard.

  1. 01

    Water often at first

    Keep new sod moist for the first couple of weeks, ideally in the morning.

  2. 02

    Stay off it

    Limit foot traffic until the roots grab the soil.

  3. 03

    Wait to mow

    Mow once it has rooted, and never cut more than a third of the blade.

  4. 04

    Ease back on water

    Water deeper and less often once it is established.
