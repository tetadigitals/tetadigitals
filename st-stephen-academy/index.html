About

Our Story

The whole child

Founded in 2013 for spirit, mind and body

St. Stephen Christian Academy was established and Accredited with Quality in 2013 as an independent Christian co-educational day school, to address the needs of the whole child: spiritual and moral growth, academic and intellectual progress, and physical and social development.

Partnering with Christian homes

We believe it is the Christian home to which God has given authority to train up successful young people. Our goal is to assist the home in that training, working closely with parents to develop every child to their fullest potential.

Our roots

From a church of 27 to an accredited academy

  1. 1949

    Church organized

    St. Stephen Missionary Baptist Church is organized July 29 with 27 members.

  2. 2005

    Multi-Family Life Center

    The Multi-Family Life Center is completed.

  3. 2009

    Saturday School Ministry begins

    Math, Science, English and Reading tutoring, plus CRCT, SAT and ACT prep.

  4. 2013

    The Academy opens

    Doors open August 12, Accredited with Quality.
